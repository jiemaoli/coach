| 单词/短语 | 中文翻译 | 英文释义 |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **1chbo, 1cb** | 首次通道突破（First Channel Breakout）。通常预期会失败，因此是朝通道方向的逆向入场信号 | The first break of a channel move. This is usually expected to fail and is a signal to enter in the direction of the channel |
| **1tf, 5tf** | 1个tick的假突破。价格超出前一根信号K线1个tick触发交易后，随即扫掉止损出场。同理5tf、9tf | 1 tick failure. Price goes 1 tick beyond prior bar triggering the trade and proceeds to take out stop. Similarly 5tf, 9tf |
| **1PB** | 当日首次回撤，或1Rev之后的首次回撤 | The first pullback in a day or the first pullback after 1Rev |
| **1Rev** | 当日首次反转。另见 OR | The first reversal of the day. Also see OR |
| **OR** | 开盘反转。开盘第一小时内出现趋势尝试后的反转 | Opening Reversal, A reversal after a trend move attempt in the first hour |
| **1L, 2L** | 单腿走势、双腿走势 | 1 legged move, 2 legged move |
| **A2** | 均线附近二次回撤入场（2 Legged move to Average）。第二次试图反转回撤失败，因此是顺势延续信号。若距均线太远则不构成有效A2 | A second attempt to reverse a pullback move near the ema. A second failed attempt to reverse the trend and therefore a continuation signal. If its too far from the ema it may not qualify to be an A2 |
| **A22** | A2的二次入场。因首次信号所需止损过大或K线重叠过多而选择第二次入场 | A second entry for an A2, taken perhaps because the first signal required a large stop or had a large overlap |
| **H1** | 下跌中K线首次触及前一根K线高点。首次尝试反转下跌 | The first time a bar ticks above the high of a previous bar in a down move. First attempt to reverse a down move |
| **fH1** | 价格跌破给出H1信号的K线低点，H1反转尝试失败 | The price falls below the bar that gave an H1, thus failing its attempt to reverse the down move |
| **fH1 MM** | 强下跌趋势中，若H1信号K线及其前一根K线均较弱，则失败H1很可能产生从走势起点到失败点的测量移动（Measured Move） | In a hard down trend, if the bar that gave an H1 and its previous bar (signal) were weak, the failed H1 is likely to give a measured move from the begin of the move to the failure |
| **H2** | H1和fH1之后，K线第二次触及前一根K线高点 | Second time a bar ticks above previous bar in a down move after the H1 and fH1 |
| **L1, fL1, L2** | 上涨趋势中的反向对应：L1=首次触及前低，fL1=触及失败，L2=第二次触及 | Inverse of H1, fH1, H2 in an up move |
| **MM** | 测量移动（Measured Move）。基于价格行为预期出现的1:1等距走势。如突破K线长度为2点，则价格大概率上涨2点 | Measured move. A 1:1 move expected due to certain price action. For example, price is likely to go 2 points above a breakout bar thats 2 points long |
| **BO** | 突破。价格突破价格行为屏障（摆动高点、均线、趋势线等） | Breakout. Price has moved beyond a price action barrier such as swing high, ema, trendline, etc |
| **BP** | 突破回撤。价格回撤至被突破的屏障内部甚至更远 | Breakout pullback. Price pulls back often back inside the price action barrier that was broken |
| **fBO** | 失败突破。大多数首次突破尝试都会失败 | A failed breakout. Most first attempts to breakout fail |
| **DP** | 双底回撤入场（做多）或双顶回撤入场（做空）。趋势生成器，本质是失败突破 | A pullback that gives a long entry after a double bottom or a short entry after a double top |
| **WP** | 三腿回撤。与A2类似 | A three legged pullback. This is similar to A2 |
| **TL** | 趋势线。上涨中连接摆动低点、下跌中连接摆动高点的直线。也可作为TCL的平行线 | Trendline. A line connecting swing lows in an up move or swing highs in a down move. Can also be a parallel line drawn to TCL |
| **TCL** | 趋势通道线（Trend Channel Line）。上涨中连接摆动高点、下跌中连接摆动低点的直线。也可作为TL的平行线，用于识别出场位置 | A line connecting swing highs in an up move or swing lows in a down move. Can also be a parallel line drawn to the TL |
| **G** | 缺口K线。下跌中第一根高点在均线下方的K线，或上涨中第一根低点在均线上方的K线。若下跌中出现阳线G，则为买入信号，反之亦然 | Gap bar. The first bar in a down move whose high is below ema or first bar in an up move whose low is above ema. If this bar is bullish in a bear move, its a buy signal and vice versa |
| **G2** | G的缺口回补尝试失败后出现的第二个G bar。胜率更高的交易 | If the G fill attempt fails, a second G bar is G2. This is a higher probability trade |
| **i, o** | 内包K线、外包K线 | inside bar, outside bar |
| **ii** | 内包套内包（三层及以上以此类推） | an inside bar inside another inside bar (also iii and so on) |
| **LL, HH** | 更低低点、更高高点 | A Lower Low, a Higher High |
| **mW** | 微型楔形（micro Wedge）。以K线实体而非摆动点连线的趋势通道线 | micro Wedge; a TCL of bars rather than swings |
| **PB** | 回撤 | pullback |
| **oio, ud** | 外包-内包-外包反转K线组合、上-下K线反转组合等 | Outside inside outside bar reversal, up down bar reversal, etc |
| **TTR** | 窄幅交易区间（Tight Trading Range）。价格密集重叠、来回反复，仅超出前一次尝试一两个tick即反转 | Tight trading range. Overlapping price movement that reverses back and forth after moving a tick or two beyond the previous attempt |
| **BW** | 铁丝网形态（Barb Wire）。三根或更多横向重叠K线，至少包含一根doji。类似TTR，本质上都是微交易区间 | Barb wire. Three or more horizontal bars with at least one being a doji. Similar to TTR |
| **W** | 楔形（Wedge）。三次上推（或下推），第三次突破趋势通道线（或至少触及）。出现反转信号即构成楔形反转 | Wedge. Three pushes up with the third push overshooting a trend channel line or at least touching it. A reversal signal makes it a W reversal |
| **W1P** | 楔形反转后首次回撤入场（Wedge 1st Pullback）。比直接交易W更安全的入场方式 | The first pullback that allows an entry in the new direction after a W reversal |
